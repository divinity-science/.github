We build AI-native systems that help people understand their health with the depth of a clinician and the clarity of a trusted advisor.

Our work sits at the intersection of longevity science, intelligent software, and human trust. We believe health data is the most personal and most underutilized asset most people have. We are building the infrastructure to change that.

<br />

## What We Build

**Health Intelligence Platform**
Personalized health analysis grounded in your labs, wearables, history, and goals. Not a dashboard. A system that reasons with you.

**Longevity Protocols**
Evidence-based frameworks for performance, recovery, and healthspan, translated into actionable workflows an individual can actually follow.

**Clinical-Grade AI Agents**
Autonomous agents that process, synthesize, and surface health insights across structured and unstructured data sources, with provenance and trust built in from the start.

<br />

## Principles

- **Clarity over complexity.** The output of analysis is a decision, not a report.
- **Trust is the product.** Every design choice is a trust choice.
- **Provenance by default.** Every insight traces to its source.
- **Education, not diagnosis.** We make people smarter about their health, not dependent on our conclusions.
- **Long-term thinking.** We build for a decade, not a demo.

<br />

## Architecture

We operate as a small, high-leverage team. Our stack is intentional and minimal:

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| Frontend | Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind |
| Backend | Python, FastAPI, LangGraph |
| Data | PostgreSQL, pgvector, Supabase |
| AI | Gemini, Claude, OpenAI — model-agnostic by design |
| Infrastructure | Vercel, Railway, GCP, GitHub Actions |
| Compliance path | HIPAA-aligned, SOC 2 in progress |
